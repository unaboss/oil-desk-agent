      How it works

      1. 1

        Analyse

        Six market signals (volume, CFTC positioning, EIA inventories, crack spread, dollar, Brent-WTI curve) plus the engine's own historical hit rate.

      2. 2

        Ask the crowd

        Polymarket odds on oil-price questions add live sentiment.

      3. 3

        Decide

        An LLM (DeepSeek V4 Flash) weighs the inputs and returns buy, sell or hold with a reason that cites its numbers. If the model is down, fixed rules take over.

      4. 4

        Act within a mandate

        The vault contract caps trade size, daily volume and slippage, and can be paused. The agent can't exceed them.

      5. 5

        Prove it

        The call, its price and the hash of the full record land on-chain in one transaction, before anyone knows the outcome.

      Honest scope: HashKey Chain has no tokenised oil yet, so v1 trades a paper oil book held in the contract at real WTI prices. The limits, the log and the track record are real. Live execution is built in: point the vault at a DEX router and the same call swaps real tokens.
